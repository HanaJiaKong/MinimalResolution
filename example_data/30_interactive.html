About this chart

The charts display the supplied MinimalResolution tables. Adams–Novikov classes are grouped using the recorded scalar towers; algebraic Novikov classes are shown individually. Missing products, uncertain orders, and unresolved additive splittings are identified in the class panel. Algebraic Novikov differentials are not Adams–Novikov differentials.

The computation is bounded by internal degree t and resolution length. Blank space beyond those bounds does not assert vanishing. Download SVG preserves the current viewport and visible layers; Data downloads the complete embedded dataset.

Horizontal grading is the stem t − s. Vertical grading is s in the Adams–Novikov view and s + i in the algebraic Novikov view. The internal-degree cutoff is always t = (t − s) + s, including when the vertical axis is s + i.
