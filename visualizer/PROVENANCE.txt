Local adaptation: offline vanilla SVG frontend with native pointer pan/zoom, chart switching, keyboard navigation and tooltips. Extended with a persistent class inspector, cyclic-summand glyphs, product filtering, search, and downloads. This is a purpose-built adaptation, not an unmodified SeqSee installation.

The converter is newly written for MinimalResolution text tables and reuses anss_chart.py for parsing and cyclic-summand grouping. It does not import the solver CSV converter or compute ANSS differentials. No dependencies on sibling checkouts, pip packages, remote scripts, fonts, or a web server.
